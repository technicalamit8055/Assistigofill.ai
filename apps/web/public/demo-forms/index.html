Practice forms for testing Assistigo. They are not connected to any portal, they submit nothing anywhere, and no real service is behind them. Use demo customer data only.

Demo forms

Open one of these, then use the Assistigo extension to detect fields and fill them.

  • Certificate application Plain English labels. The simplest case.
  • Scholarship application Hindi and English labels, a CAPTCHA box and an OTP box — both must be skipped.
  • Recruitment application Photo and signature uploads, and a state → district dependent dropdown.
  • Unsupported form Deliberately odd labels, to test the low-confidence path.

What to check

  • The selected customer's name and mobile last four stay visible throughout.
  • Every field shows a confidence score, and low-confidence ones need confirmation.
  • The CAPTCHA and OTP boxes are reported as skipped, and stay empty.
  • Nothing is ever submitted — the submit button is never clicked.
  • Filled / skipped / needs-review counts appear before and after the fill.
